✦ Where they live

Your pillar of light

Eleven of your chakras sit within your body, from the base of your spine to the top of your head. Three rise above you: the Causal, Soul Star and Stellar Gateway. The Earth Star rests about 12 inches beneath your feet.

The Zeal Point sits at the back of your head, where the skull meets the neck, and the Causal sits slightly behind your crown, so both are shown a little off the center line.

Tap any point to open its lesson.

✦ Three steps to balance your pillar

Assess, Align, Activate

Inspired by my teacher, Athena Perrakis, in The Ultimate Guide to Chakras.

Come back to these whenever you feel more tired, sad or restless than usual, or you can't sleep through the night. None of this happens overnight. We drift out of balance slowly, over years of not paying attention, so we come back the same way: one check-in at a time. Take what resonates, and always trust your own knowing above anyone's guidance, including mine.

✦ Open sacred space · Calling in the directions

Sylvia's invocation, inspired by Athena Perrakis and Steve Nobel's work with the archangels. She channels it fresh each time, so let your own words come through too.

As you call each one, welcome them in: "Archangel Raphael, you are welcome to join us in the East."

East · Archangel Raphael. The green ray of healing, the caduceus, new beginnings, the element of air and the eagle.

South · Archangel Michael. The blue ray of protection, courage and strength, the element of fire, the panther and the lion.

West · Archangel Gabriel. The diamond light, the grandmother, our emotions flowing through us like the element of water.

North · Archangel Uriel. The yellow ray, the grandfather redwood tree, the bear and the buffalo, the element of earth.

Below · Archangel Sandalphon. The creepy crawlies, the crystal and mineral kingdom, and the bones of the ancestors we stand upon.

Above · Archangel Metatron. Opening our central column of light, free and clear of distortion, connected down through and around us, and connecting us to our higher self and to Source, Creator of all that is.

As above, so below. Amen, A'ho, and so it is. It is done.

✦ Close sacred space · Dismissing the directions

When your session or energy work is complete, dismiss each archangel and thank them for their service to the circle, unwinding the circle in the reverse of the order you called them in: Metatron (Above), Sandalphon (Below), Uriel (North), Gabriel (West), Michael (South), and Raphael (East) last. "Archangel Metatron, thank you for your service to our circle. You are dismissed with love."

1

Assess

Softly say your name a few times to call your energy into the present. Call in your guides and open sacred space with the directions above.

Scan slowly from your feet to the top of your head, thanking each part of your body as you go. There's nothing to fix, only to notice.

Then ask: "Show me where my attention is needed." Note any sensation, image, sound or feeling, and rate it from 0 to 10. Keep noticing for the next two days.

2

Align

Choose one intention, such as peace, love or prosperity, and see it as a golden needle threaded with light.

Wrap the golden thread around your Earth Star, then weave it up through each chakra on your pillar: Root, Sacral, Navel, Solar Plexus, Heart and on up, to your Soul Star and Stellar Gateway.

Let each center receive your intention as the thread passes through, and rest in the peace at the top.

3

Activate

Imagine your sushumna, the central column of your pillar, as a tube of light. Open it below your feet and above your head.

Move up the pillar one chakra at a time, saying "Access, open, activate" at each one.

When you reach the top, give thanks and close the column at both ends. Amen, A'ho, so be it and so it is.

Your energy is always flowing. It runs up and down your pillar and then curves out and around you in a toroidal field, like a very large donut of light. Its size is measured in feet or in meters, depending on where people are from.
